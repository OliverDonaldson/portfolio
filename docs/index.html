Biography

Hi, I'm Ollie — a third-year Data Science student at Victoria University of Wellington, with a foundation in Legal Studies that still shapes how I think about data privacy and AI governance. I build end-to-end systems: the data path, the model, the thing that serves it, and the honest account of where it falls over.

Over the 2025/26 summer I interned at Harmoney, where I replaced a manual loan-closure process handling ~70–80 requests a month with an automated workflow, and built Snowflake reporting over raw MongoDB and Kafka data for the Finance team. Outside of study I run Exoplanet Hunter, a deployed platform that ranks NASA transit candidates by calibrated probability — built around a promotion gate that has rejected far more of my ideas than it has accepted.

I'm on the general committee of VicAI, VUW's student AI society, and I'm open to internships and graduate roles where I can keep building things that get used.
